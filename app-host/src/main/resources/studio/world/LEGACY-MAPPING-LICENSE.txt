Legacy schematic block-state data

Source: PrismarineJS/minecraft-data
Revision: 11fed0c9126d8a4f9a72ee4809f66d996f503688
https://github.com/PrismarineJS/minecraft-data

legacy-blockstates.json contains the blocks portion of
  data/pc/common/legacy.json
and solid connector IDs derived from boundingBox/transparent in
  data/pc/1.12/blocks.json
with double slabs included and gourd/barrier connectors excluded.
Modern names/properties and neighbor-derived preview states are handled
by LegacyBlockStates.java, not downloaded or executed at runtime.
